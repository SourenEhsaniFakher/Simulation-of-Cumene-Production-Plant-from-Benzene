### Simulation of Cumene Production Plant from Benzene

**Description:**
Developed a steady-state simulation for the industrial production of Cumene (isopropylbenzene) from Benzene and Propylene using Aspen HYSYS. The project focused on process flow diagram (PFD) development, mass and energy balancing, and the optimization of chemical conversion and product purification.

**Key Highlights:**
* **Reactor Network Design:** Configured and optimized both **alkylation and transalkylation reactors** to maximize Cumene yield, manage highly exothermic reactions, and minimize the formation of undesirable byproducts (such as DIPB).
* **Process Simulation & Optimization:** Rigorously modeled the entire chemical process flowsheet in **Aspen HYSYS**, selecting appropriate thermodynamic fluid packages to accurately predict phase equilibria.
* **Separation & Recovery:** Implemented a downstream separation section involving distillation columns for the high-purity recovery of the final Cumene product and the recycling of unreacted Benzene back into the process loop.

**Tools & Technologies:** Aspen HYSYS, Chemical Process Simulation, Reactor Design, Thermodynamics, Steady-State Modeling
